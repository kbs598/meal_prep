# Recipe sources and attribution

Weeknight Five includes 90 mild, celiac-aware recipe adaptations inspired by TheMealDB and the Wikibooks Cookbook. The app does not copy source directions or photos. Its ingredient lists, short directions, family notes, and nutrition estimates were newly written or substantially adapted for this project. Every adapted recipe has a source-inspiration link in the recipe details.

## TheMealDB

Sixty-four recipe concepts were selected through [TheMealDB's official API](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php). The source meal ID is stored with each entry in `R/expanded_recipes.R`, and the app links directly to the corresponding TheMealDB page. TheMealDB describes its database as open and crowd-sourced and offers its API for development and personal projects. No source directions or images are bundled here.

## Wikibooks Cookbook

Twenty-six adaptations were inspired by pages in the [Wikibooks Cookbook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Table_of_Contents). Wikibooks text is available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and, unless otherwise noted, the GNU Free Documentation License. Page histories linked from each source page identify the contributors.

The Wikibooks-derived recipe data and adaptations in `R/expanded_recipes.R` are made available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes include gluten-free substitutions, removal of spicy ingredients, shorter original directions, family-sized quantities, meal-type classification, and estimated nutrition.

## Safety and nutrition note

“Gluten-free” in this app describes the recipe as written, not a guarantee about a specific product or kitchen. For celiac safety, check labels, use certified gluten-free products where indicated, and prevent cross-contact. Nutrition figures are estimates per serving and should not be used as medical calculations.
